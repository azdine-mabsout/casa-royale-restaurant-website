Our Story

A Taste of Elegance in the Heart of Casablanca

Perched along the Atlantic crest of Boulevard de la Corniche, Casa Royale was born from an unyielding pursuit of gastronomic grace. Here, the ancestral hospitality of Morocco intertwines with timeless European culinary savoir-faire.

Every creation pays homage to the bounties of our realm: wild Atlantic sea bass brought from coastal fleets at dawn, mountain-foraged thyme and saffron from the High Atlas ranges, and heirloom produce nurtured by Moroccan smallholders. Under hand-crafted cedar arches and soft ambient lighting, each dinner unfolds as an intimate chapter in Moroccan modern luxury.
